# Japanese words

Each section eyebrow shows a brush numeral, a rule, the label in the site's language and a kanji name. Use the words below when they fit. For anything else, ask the user, or show the numeral alone. A wrong kanji is worse than none.

## Numerals

一 二 三 四 五 六 七 八 九 十 (1–10). Number sections in page order and skip the hero and footer. For years and dates in festive designs, 〇 is zero: 二〇二六 = 2026.

## Seals and big words

- **Hanko**: two to four kana of the brand name, set vertically. Kana can spell any name (Tomari → とまり, Kumo → くも, Lena → レナ). Use hiragana for a soft, native feel and katakana for foreign names, and confirm the reading with the user.
- **Interlude word**: one meaningful noun shown huge in brush type, with its romaji reading and a dictionary-style gloss (`ma · noun · the pause between things`). Choose a word that names the site's idea, such as 間 ma, 縁 en (connection), 和 wa (harmony), 一期一会 ichigo ichie (a once-in-a-lifetime meeting), 木漏れ日 komorebi (sunlight through leaves) or 止まり木 tomarigi (a perch), and confirm it with the user.
